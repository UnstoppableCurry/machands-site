Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-04 · Controller / seller: Tianxin Wang (individual), MacHands / MacDisk AI

1. Scope

This policy describes how the macOS app MacDisk AI (外盘智管; bundle ID com.macdisk.ai) handles information when you install and use it, and how the seller handles information if you purchase a subscription or email support.

2. Data processed on your Mac (local)

Most processing happens on your device:

  • Paths and metadata of folders/files you scan or select — to build relocation candidates. Stored in local app support files.
  • Expansion volume identity (volume UUID) — to remember your chosen external disk.
  • Move / journal / audit logs — for copy → verify → replace and restore. Local store and journal on the expansion volume.
  • Optional planner API key — macOS Keychain only, when you save it.
  • Subscription entitlement state — StoreKit or the payment provider’s license signal.

Full Disk Access, if granted, is used to scan reclaimable user data you choose to manage — not to exfiltrate passwords. The app does not read Mail or Messages content.

3. Network

  • Default / offline: Heuristic planning can run without sending file contents to us.
  • Optional AI planner: If you enable it and provide an API key, plan requests go from your Mac to that provider under their privacy terms.
  • Purchases: Apple, Paddle, PayPal, or another processor receives payment data under their privacy policy. We may receive your email, order ID, and subscription status to deliver support and entitlements.

4. What the seller collects

If you email support or complete a purchase outside the App Store, we may receive the email address and message you send, plus order / subscription metadata from the payment provider (not full card numbers). We do not sell personal data.

5. Retention

Local app data remains on your devices until you delete the app or clear its support files / Keychain entry. Support emails are kept as long as needed to resolve your request. Payment providers retain transaction records per their policies and law.

6. Your choices

  • Do not grant Full Disk Access if you do not want broad scanning.
  • Leave Dry-run enabled until you are ready to move data.
  • Do not save an API key if you do not want third-party planner calls.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through the channel you used (Paddle, PayPal, or Apple).

7. Children

The Software is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).
