2. What the Software does

MacHands is a native macOS utility that helps you free space on an internal SSD by relocating user-selected, low-priority (“Safe”-tier) data to a volume you designate, while leaving a symlink or stub portal at the original path so many apps and workflows can keep working. It is a path-preserving storage relocation tool, not a junk cleaner, optimizer, or antivirus.
