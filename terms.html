2. What the Software does

MacHands is a native macOS menu-bar utility that helps you free space on an internal SSD by relocating user-selected, low-priority (“Safe”-tier) data to a volume you designate, while leaving a symlink or stub portal at the original path so many apps and workflows can keep working. It is a path-preserving storage relocation tool, not a junk cleaner, optimizer, or antivirus.

You remain responsible for choosing which items to relocate, verifying backups, and granting only the permissions you are comfortable with (including Full Disk Access if you enable scanning of protected locations).
