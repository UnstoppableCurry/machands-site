MacHands

Keep a small internal SSD usable by relocating data to your external volume — without breaking original paths.

Also known as 外盘智管. Native macOS menu-bar utility. Chinese-primary UI, English secondary.

  • Watch free space on the internal drive. When it drops below your threshold, plan a relocation.
  • You choose what moves. Safe-tier items include old downloads, Xcode DerivedData / DeviceSupport, package and browser caches.
  • Copy to your designated external SSD, verify size, then leave a symlink (folders) or stub portal (apps) at the original path so workflows keep working.
  • If the external volume is unplugged, automatic relocation pauses and already-moved apps tell you to reconnect — they do not crash.
  • Dry-run first. One-click restore brings data back and removes the link.
  • This is a path-preserving storage relocation tool. It is not a junk cleaner, optimizer, antivirus, or “speed up your Mac” utility.
