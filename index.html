How it works

Three clear steps. You stay in control of every move.

1

Choose your external volume

Pick the SSD once. MacHands remembers it by volume UUID and watches free space on your internal drive.

2

Review Safe-tier candidates

Old downloads, Xcode DerivedData / DeviceSupport, caches — you decide what moves. Dry-run first, always.

3

Relocate with paths intact

Data is copied, verified, then replaced with a symlink or stub portal. Original paths keep working. One-click restore when you need it back.

FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

Is this a junk cleaner?

No. MacHands is a path-preserving storage relocation tool. It moves data you choose to an external volume and leaves links at the original paths. It is not an optimizer, antivirus, or “speed up your Mac” utility.

What happens if I unplug the external disk?

Automatic relocation pauses. Already-moved items that need the volume will prompt you to reconnect — they should not crash the app that opens them.

Do I need Full Disk Access?

Only if you want scans of protected locations. You can use MacHands with narrower access; grant FDA only when you are comfortable.
